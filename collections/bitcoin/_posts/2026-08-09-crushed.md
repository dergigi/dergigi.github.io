My thoughts around the recent catastrophe are still evolving. The dust hasn't
settled yet; neither have all transactions that are moving funds into attackers'
wallets (It's safe to say that [not all of them](https://haven.dergigi.com/3ff9e103ae559c8d7c0d7ae158b3bfae680e28dec199a68bf6ed67528930d55a.htm) were even broadcast yet). And
while I've broadcast [GM](https://gm.dergigi.com/) and [GN](https://gn.dergigi.com/) multiple times, I have yet to get a full night's
sleep.

It was a weird couple of days, to put it mildly. For thousands (tens of thousands?)
of people, but also for me. [Funeral](/2026/06/21/sayings/). Emergency travel.
Making sure that me and my family are okay. Trying to do what I can to help
others.

I'm sitting in an airplane as I'm typing this, and I can't help but think of the
one line with the oxygen mask. [Last weekend](/2026/08/03/the-big-suck/) was like that. "We've lost cabin
pressure. Put your own mask on first, and after that try to help others."

I don't even want to imagine all the scenarios. People on holidays. People in
[hospitals](/2026/06/17/cancer/). The proverbial guy in the coma. Friends or acquaintances breaking
into people's homes, offices, garages, safe deposit boxes, and so forth. People
flooring it on the highway to get to their keys. People who lost it all. How
many of them are thinking about [ending their life](https://njump.to/nevent1qqswvqrp4af7exaxhghhue87ghwnz64v7cvf4kyuv7fa8dqtpum2fjgmdmgqn) right now? When the damage is
100+ million and the affected are in the thousands, I'm afraid it's [more than
zero](/suicide).

And then there's the 2nd and 3rd order effects. People losing it all because
they make a mistake, mess up their new setup, or are getting scammed. And the
ecosystem fallout, and so on and so forth. Brutal.

---

I keep thinking about the spiritual damage that was and is being done. I've
never been a fan of the word "spiritual," but I can't find a better word for
what is happening. A lot of people are crushed right now. Spiritually crushed.

Friends, colleagues, people I look up to. Absolutely crushed by this. Feeling
responsible. "How could I miss this?" "Why didn't I look?" "How could I ever
recommend a coldcard?" "Why did I trust this company to such an extent?" "Why
didn't I roll dice / why didn't I tell others to roll the dice?"

Oh, the dice. I'm not sure how I feel about the dice thing to be honest, even
though I'm somewhat of a dice roller myself. There is big victim blaming energy
in the dice roll discussion. *"Why didn't you roll the dice, bro? You should've
rolled the dice."*

Yeah, no. I don't think so. The damn thing should've done what it is supposed to
do, and the low-entropy path should never have been allowed to be there in the
first place.

Speaking of which: [James](https://xcancel.com/jamesob/status/2084624605969350915) has
responsibly disclosed that there might be an issue on the RNG side, as have
others before him. How is it possible that these disclosures weren't taken
seriously? How can you receive multiple disclosures, as well as reports from
customers, and dismiss them all? I can't blame the conspiracy theorists for
putting on an additional layer of tinfoil.

---

And yet, Bitcoin is fine. It was one line of code. One vendor. One set of
firmware versions. One type of hardware wallet. One CEO. One company. One
developer.

That's what bothers me the most right now: [one developer](https://haven.dergigi.com/2e612f3b938d0bf9f3130d308d0780ae969189ea672e1a19d88b6574825fab68.htm). Like, what the fuck?
How can the development process of a company be this negligent? No review?
[No proper commit messages?](https://haven.dergigi.com/ea44d78e0c1c08248dc78044c5fa278bc94514f84ad0851d0f85262b5ba5d6f9.htm) No
making sure that the most important code path of the whole thing actually
executes? And once more: why is there a low-entropy fallback in the first
place? All the fancy features don't matter if the main functionality is
broken.

---

My [first reaction](/2026/08/03/the-big-suck/) to this mess[^fn-name] was one of regret: I wrote about
[this exact issue](https://21lessons.com/16/), the issue of [entropy](/2019/12/22/bitcoin-s-eternal-struggle/#entropy), many times in the past. There's a bunch
of people who have read my stuff, and I know of multiple people who
were unaffected by this exploit because of it. I stopped writing, however, and
the new cohort of bitcoiners never had much exposure to my writing. I also
regret not having a closer look at the codebase myself. Ah, regret. Painful,
painful regret.

Anyway. I'm writing all this, at least in part, to see if I can trick myself
into writing again. (I'll try.)

---

So, where do we go from here? And the answer I'm looking for isn't "dice rolls"
or "multi-vendor multisig."

Where do we go from here, spiritually? What is the proper path forward? What is
[the lesson](https://21lessons.com) we can learn from all this? How are we going to integrate this
catastrophe into our collective unconscious? Will there be a transmutation, or
will most people just move on to the next thing and put their trust in some
other company or device?

My heart goes out to anyone who has lost their savings, their sanity, or worse.
To those who are now broke or broken, be it financially, spiritually, or
otherwise.

Bad times. [Bad seeds](/2026/08/06/bad-seeds/). Bad situation.

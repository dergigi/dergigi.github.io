### Fly Pingu Fly

A tiny one-button ski-jump game. Slide the penguin to the flag to start, time the takeoff, and try to beat your longest jump. Concept and game design by my daughter.

### Unicorn vs Snakes

A kid-friendly pixel sidescroller. Guide a brave unicorn through forest, lava, and castle levels, collecting sparkles and dodging snakes. Nostr-powered leaderboard for speedruns. Built together with my daughter.
